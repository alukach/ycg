If the flight is cancelled Air Canada's shuttle via Kelowna

When weather makes a Castlegar landing unlikely, Air Canada usually cancels before take-off and offers a free, optional shuttle through Kelowna (YLW), at its discretion. Some passengers, such as unaccompanied minors, may not be eligible. Rebooking for another day is still an option.

  • Flying in from Vancouver: you're offered the switch at the gate. Air Canada flies you to Kelowna, and a bus leaves in the early afternoon for Castlegar, about 350 km and 3.5–4 h away. Plan on reaching YCG around 7:30–8 p.m.
  • Flying out of Castlegar: the bus leaves YCG 45 min before the scheduled departure and reaches Kelowna 3.5–4 h later. Air Canada rebooks your onward flights from there.
  • Picking someone up? On a shuttle day, they arrive by bus in the evening, not at 10:14.

Track record, Dec 2023 – Sep 2024: 84% of flights operated; counting the shuttle, 97% of trips were completed the same day (Nelson Star).

    How the outlook works
    
    
    The cancellation outlook is a hand-tuned heuristic, not an airline forecast. It starts from a base rate, which is the share of arrivals cancelled or diverted over the trailing 30 days blended with a seasonal prior (the prior dominates until about a month of history exists), and adds risk for low ceilings, poor visibility, fog, snow, freezing precipitation and strong gusts at the scheduled YCG time, using the latest METAR when it is within ~75 minutes, the TAF when it covers the flight, and the Open-Meteo model otherwise. The departure (AC8377) uses the same aircraft as the inbound AC8376, so its risk follows the arrival until that aircraft is on the ground. Live airline status (cancelled, diverted, arrived) always overrides the model.
    
    
    Context: Castlegar's day-only approach uses an unusually steep glideslope through mountainous terrain (Wikipedia). Air Canada reported an 84% landing/take-off success rate on the Vancouver route between December 2023 and September 2024, with a bus shuttle via Kelowna on cancellation days (Nelson Star). The seasonal base rates are estimates, not published figures; see MONTH_BASE in wx.js.
